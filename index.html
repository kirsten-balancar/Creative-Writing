01 — The Leader

Vince Kirsten B. Balgomia

known as “Kirsten”

Grade 12 · HUMSS
Judge Feliciano Belmonte Sr. High School

“With a scientist's mind, a writer's voice, and a leader's heart.”

Kirsten writes the way a researcher takes notes — carefully, and with the sense that everything observed is worth recording. Across his senior high school years he has moved easily between disciplines that don't often share a desk: literary composition on one page, statistical inquiry on the next, a poem about the sky on the one after that. It isn't range for its own sake. It's one mind working the same questions — what's true, what's overlooked, what deserves a second look — through whatever form fits the question best.

He is drawn to astronomy the way some writers are drawn to the sea: as a fixed point that makes everything else easier to measure. That instinct shows up across his collection, in essays that reach for the stars to talk about identity and in reflections that turn a critical eye on the everyday. He leads the way he writes — by paying attention first, and speaking second.
