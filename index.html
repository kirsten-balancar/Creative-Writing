Creative Writing: Creating a Literary Online Portfolio Using Multimedia Forms

This portfolio presents the creative works of Group 3 through literary writing, visual design, multimedia elements, and digital hyperlinks. Rather than presenting the works as plain text alone, each piece is given a visual atmosphere designed to complement its subject, emotion, and message.

MULTIMEDIA • 01

Words become an experience.

Literature can be presented through more than written words. Color, typography, imagery, movement, layout, and digital links can work together to create a more immersive literary experience.

CLARK DELOS REYES

The Blue Flowers

MEMBER WORK • 01 Short Story

The Blue Flowers

by Clark Delos Reyes

The Story

Maya discovers a patch of blue flowers beside an old tree and learns from a gardener that their beauty comes from constant care. Inspired by his words, she begins helping him and sees how small acts of kindness can brighten an entire community.

Essence

The story centers on kindness, patience, care, and hope. It shows how small everyday actions can create beauty and resilience even in difficult circumstances.

A Quiet Kind of Love

MEMBER WORK • 02 Poem

A Quiet Kind of Love

by Rezielyn Pestilos

The Poem

A reflection on a gentle kind of love that grows through simple moments, quiet presence, kindness, trust, and sincerity rather than dramatic declarations.

Essence

The poem presents love as a conscious and everyday choice: a commitment to care, stay, understand, and remain sincere through life's changing seasons.

CRISTINA PAGADUAN

The Road Only I Could See

MEMBER WORK • 03 Poem

The Road Only I Could See

by Cristina Pagaduan
Every journey begins with a single step, even when the destination is uncertain.

The Poem

A journey through fear, uncertainty, scars, hardship, and the gradual discovery of strength. The speaker learns that every step and every struggle can become part of the map toward becoming who they are meant to be.

Essence

The poem explores resilience and self-discovery, transforming darkness and uncertainty into morning clarity, hope, and personal growth.

JAMES LUCERO

Admired Only

MEMBER WORK • 05 Creative Prose

Admired Only

by James Lucero

The Piece

A reflection on distant affection, comparing the beauty of a person to the ocean, moonlight, and stars. The piece explores the feeling of admiring someone without needing to possess or claim them.

Essence

The work presents love as appreciation rather than ownership. Some people may be meant to be admired and cherished from afar, just as the stars can inspire wonder without ever being touched.
