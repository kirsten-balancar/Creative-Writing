01

The Blue Flowers

by Clark Delos Reyes
Short story + visual elements

The Blue Flowers is a short story about Maya, a young student who discovers a garden that teaches her the importance of kindness, patience, and hope. Its watercolor-inspired visuals and blue floral elements support the peaceful message that small acts of care can create something beautiful and inspire a community.

Multimedia Elements

  • Written narrative
  • Watercolor-style background
  • Blue floral imagery
  • Decorative composition and typography
  • Soft blue and silver-purple tones

Short Presentation

“Our first work is The Blue Flowers, a short story about how small acts of kindness and care can create something meaningful. The watercolor-style visuals and blue floral elements support its peaceful and hopeful message.”

02

A Quiet Kind of Love

by Rezielyn Pestilos
Poem + visual elements

A Quiet Kind of Love is a poem about finding genuine love in simple moments, kindness, trust, and quiet companionship. Its vintage-inspired visuals, parchment textures, romantic landscape, roses, and sepia imagery reinforce the idea that lasting love is not about grand gestures, but about choosing to care, stay, and love each day.

Multimedia Elements

  • Written poetry
  • Romantic landscape painting
  • Aged parchment and scrapbook textures
  • Red roses and floral details
  • Sepia couple imagery
  • Glowing script typography

Short Presentation

“Our second work is A Quiet Kind of Love, a poem that presents love as something found in everyday moments rather than grand gestures. The vintage visuals, romantic landscape, parchment textures, roses, and sepia imagery support its message of kindness, trust, and commitment.”

03

The Road Only I Could See

by Cristina Pagaduan
Poem + visual elements

The Road Only I Could See is a reflective poem about facing fear, hardship, and uncertainty while discovering personal strength. Its visuals move from a dark, foggy road into warm morning light, symbolizing the journey from doubt and struggle toward hope, growth, and self-discovery.

Multimedia Elements

  • Written poetry
  • Foggy forest road
  • Nighttime streetlights
  • Golden morning light
  • Visual transition from darkness to warmth

Short Presentation

“Our third work is The Road Only I Could See by Cristina Pagaduan. The poem uses a journey along an uncertain road as a metaphor for personal growth. Its visuals transition from darkness into morning light, reflecting the movement from fear toward hope and self-discovery.”

04

Mountstrain

by Abby Stephanie Sta. Maria
Poem + visual elements

Mountstrain is an environmental poem about pollution and plastic waste and their effects on natural landscapes and communities. Its forest imagery and vibrant greenery emphasize the beauty of nature while supporting the poem's urgent call to protect it before that beauty is lost.

Multimedia Elements

  • Written poetry
  • Forest canopy and jungle imagery
  • Floating bokeh and light effects
  • Natural green and warm tones
  • Cursive title typography

Short Presentation

“Our fourth work is Mountstrain by Abby Stephanie Sta. Maria. The poem focuses on pollution and plastic waste and how they damage the natural environment. Its forest imagery emphasizes nature's beauty while the poem calls on people to protect it before that beauty is lost.”
